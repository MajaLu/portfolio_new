I’m a Junior Data Analyst who likes asking questions, spotting patterns, and making sense of messy data (because it rarely arrives neatly organized!). I use Python, pandas, NumPy, SQL and SQLite to clean and explore data, and statistical and machine-learning methods to dig a little deeper. I also enjoy turning the results into clear visuals and dashboards with Matplotlib, Seaborn, Tableau and Streamlit.

I’m drawn to questions about unusual behavior, changing trends, and the factors behind an outcome. The best part is when a confusing pile of numbers finally starts to tell a story. Away from my laptop, you’ll usually find me exploring nature with my dog, listening to music, or keeping active. I’m always open to connecting, sharing ideas, and hearing about interesting data questions—feel free to say hello!
